Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF)

AgriFlow LBM

Research platform for agricultural hydrology, soil physics, and porous-media flow.

Developed as part of ASPF scientific computing work. The numerical engine is implemented in Fortran. All models, algorithms, formulations, and source remain confidential.

Scientific research focus

Soil-water dynamics

Fluid movement through agricultural porous media to support precision irrigation and nutrient management.

Hydrological modelling

Numerical frameworks for water flow, retention, and transport in soil systems.

Porous-media physics

Pore-scale processes that govern macroscopic hydraulic properties of agricultural soils.

Transport processes

Nutrient and solute movement under agricultural conditions.

Research platform

AgriFlow LBM is a privately developed scientific computing platform under active research at Agriculture Service of Product Farm. Status: active research and development. Access is limited to the private research repository. Public disclosure of numerical models, algorithms, or source code is not provided.

  • Numerical engine implemented in Fortran.
  • Local high-performance computation environment.
  • No scientific formulations or source code published on this site.
  • Human approval and traceability requirements apply to any integration.
