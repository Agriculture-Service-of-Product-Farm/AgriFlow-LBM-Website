Scientific Research Focus

Soil-Water Dynamics

Investigating fluid movement through agricultural porous media to support precision irrigation and nutrient management.

Developing numerical frameworks for understanding water flow, retention, and transport in soil systems.

Characterizing pore-scale processes that govern macroscopic hydraulic properties of agricultural soils.
