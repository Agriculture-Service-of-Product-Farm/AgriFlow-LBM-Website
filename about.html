About AgriFlow LBM

AgriFlow LBM is a research platform developed by Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) as part of its scientific computing and agricultural engineering programme.

It supports research in agricultural hydrology, soil physics, porous-media flow, and precision irrigation systems. The numerical engine is implemented in Fortran. All models, algorithms, formulations, and source code remain confidential and are not disclosed on this public site.
